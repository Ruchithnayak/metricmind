# Decision Engine

MetricMind NOVA converts governed metrics into decision signals through a transparent sequence:

1. Validate the available records.
2. Aggregate revenue, profit, net revenue, units and discount rate.
3. Compare margin against the 25% management target.
4. Rank regions, categories and countries.
5. Identify the weakest margin signal.
6. Generate a recommended investigation or scenario.
7. Expose the calculation trace to the user.

The browser implementation is local-first and does not claim predictive accuracy where historical backtesting is unavailable.
