# Metrics Catalog

| Metric | Definition | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Sum of transaction revenue | Scale |
| Net Revenue | Revenue minus discount | Realized sales |
| Profit | Net revenue minus cost and shipping | Economics |
| Margin | Profit / net revenue | Efficiency |
| Discount Rate | Discount / revenue | Leakage |
| Units | Sum of units sold | Volume |

All decision surfaces should reference governed definitions rather than reimplementing formulas independently.
